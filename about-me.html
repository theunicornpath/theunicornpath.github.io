About Me!

My name is Delta, I'm 26 years old. My Sun sign is Gemini, my Moon is Aquarius, and my Rising is Libra. I have two stelliums -- one in Cancer and one in Aquarius.

I am a High Priestess of the Unicorn Path. This space exists as an archive of everything I've learned, gathered, and been shown along my own journey into unicorn magic ; a practice I hold as real, sacred, and far deeper than the myths most people grew up with.

Everything you'll find here; the rituals, the symbols, the correspondences, the alchemy all comes from my own study, practice, and intuition, along with resources I've collected from others walking similar paths. I share it not as absolute doctrine, but as an invitation. Take what resonates. Question what doesn't. Let your own discernment be your guide.

Alongside my work on the Unicorn Path, I offer my gifts as an intuitive and tarot reader. For as long as I can remember, I've sensed things before I could explain them, a knowing about how something would unfold, a feeling in a room that words couldn't quite understand. Over time, I learned to trust that sense instead of talking myself out of it, and to build a practice around it.

In the spirit of respect, reciprocity, and truth, I honor and acknowledge that the land where I live, create, and guide my spiritual practice is the traditional and ancestral territory of many Indigenous peoples who have cared for these lands since time immemorial. I respectfully acknowledge that the province of Alberta encompasses the traditional lands and diverse territories of Treaty 6, Treaty 7, and Treaty 8, as well as portions of Treaty 4 and Treaty 10. These lands are the ancestral homes, sacred gathering grounds, and travelling routes of many First Nations, including the Nehiyawak (Cree), Siksikaitsitapi (Blackfoot Confederacy), Denesųłıné, Tsuut’ina, Îyârhe Nakoda, Anishinaabe, Haudenosaunee, and Saulteaux peoples. I also recognize that these regions are the historic Northwest Métis homeland, and I honor the Otipemisiwak Métis Government alongside the diverse Métis Districts and Settlements whose connections to the land run deep.
