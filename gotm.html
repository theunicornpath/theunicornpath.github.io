Guardians of the Meadow!

Over my years of working with Unicorns, I have consistently heard the term “Guardians of the Meadow” to refer to high ranking Elemental Unicorns. Elemental unicorns are different from Guardian Unicorns because they rule over an element within their realm, and thus an epithet in our realm – Earth.

These beings are the guardians of their specific element, and thus can be called upon by anyone. You can call upon them in ritual, or prayer/meditation to evoke the Unicorn Element’s, or just to test the waters with unicorn magic in general.

As I’ve been working with unicorns for years, I have come to discover the following Guardians, and hope you can connect with them as well. The Guardians of the Meadow are the original ancestors of all unicorns, pegasus and alicorns directly descended from the primal stock.

The Guardians

  • ,Air: Master of the skies and the air element. Rides around in air currents with its large wings. This creature is the mother of all pegasi, and its creators are sometimes accredited to Poseidon and Medusa.
  • Fire: Masters of heat and flame, tasked with keeping the hearth lit. This creature is often depicted having dragon-like scales, and may be a hybrid.
  • Water: Ruler of water, has a fish tail, connected to sea horses. Controls the tides of their realm. One of the guardians of the birthing grotto where new foals are born to keep them safe.
  • Earth: Commander the solid ground, master of grounding and nature magic.
  • Spirit: Navigates the unseen world, bridging the gap between the unseen world and consciousness to keep unicorn-kind and their humans guided and safe.
  • Light: Conjurer of pure, light energy. Their horn cuts through darkness and pierces through shadows. Brings back hope when things get heavy. – Keeps Shadow in balance.
  • Dark / Shadow: Shadowwork guide, can slip through the dark unseen, able to help in hard moments, makes sure the natural cycle is in check – keeps Light in balance
  • Death: Guardian of endings and big transitions, keeping the natural world growing and renewing
  • Life: Packed with creation energy, makes sure new things come to fruition, keeps their realm renewed and growing
  • Chaos: Wild, unpredictable magic. Shifting sands and breaking up boring patterns. Keeps their realm from being stuck in a rut.
  • Order: Masters of balance, guardians of the cosmic law of their realm, calms down wild magics, and builds strong boundaries to keep everything running smoothly
